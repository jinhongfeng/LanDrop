# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People on the same Wi-Fi who need to move files or text between phones, laptops, and desktop computers without signing in, installing a cloud client, or sending data through a third-party service. They are usually in a desk, meeting room, dorm, home, repair shop, or shared office context and want the transfer to finish in a few seconds.

## Product Purpose

LanDrop provides a small local web service for same-network transfers. Any device can open the LAN URL, join a room by QR code or six-digit code, then send text, clipboard content, and files to every other connected device in that room. Success means the user can start the server on one computer, connect another phone or computer, and complete a transfer without account setup or cloud storage.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, capable, everyday. The interface should feel like a trusted utility: clear status, direct actions, and no decorative friction.

## Anti-references

Avoid cloud-drive dashboards, account-first onboarding, huge landing pages, fake productivity metrics, playful gimmicks, and heavy dark control panels. This is not a SaaS marketing surface; it is a task tool.

## Design Principles

Make the first transfer obvious.

Treat phone-to-computer, computer-to-phone, and computer-to-computer as the same workflow.

Show local network facts plainly: LAN address, room code, connection count, and transfer state.

Keep data local by default and make that promise visible through behavior, not slogans.

Prefer compact controls over explanation-heavy panels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, touch targets of at least 44px on mobile, reduced-motion support, and resilient Chinese/English/CJK text wrapping. The app should remain usable when clipboard permissions are denied or when a QR scanner is unavailable.
